Myths VS Facts
MYTHS FACTS
You only use 10% of your brain. Brain imaging proves we use virtually all parts of our brain, and it remains active around the clock.
People are strictly "left brained" or "right" brained. Both hemisphere work together for complex tasks like creativity and problem solving.
Adult brain cannot grow new cells. The brain has neuroplasticity (the ability to reorganise) and can generate new neurons through a procss called as neurogenesis.
The brain works completely alone. The brain constantly communicates with the rest of the nervous system.
