Brain's Learning and Memory

Have You Ever Wondered how your brain learn something new and somehow remember it later? How does your brain know what to remember,what to forget,and how to bring a memory back when you need it.

Well!Let's Find Out

If we define learning broadly as the acquisition of knowledge or skills (either through intructuion,study,or experience)then the acquired knowledgeor skill must be stored somehow in the brain for recall and use later.This,of course,is the role of memory.Therefore,learning and memory are closely linked in thr brain.

How Does The Brain Learn?

  • Brain receives new information
  • It processes and connects it with what you already know
  • Practice can strengthen these connections

Meet The Neurons

Neurons are specialised cells that communicate information in the brain. Their connections play an important role in learning.

What Is Memory

Memory helps your brain store and retrieve information when you need it.

Short-Term vs Long-Term
Type Meaning
Short-Term Holds information temporarily
Long-Term Stores information for a much longer time

Learning+Memory

Learning gives you new information,while memory helps you keep and use it later.

Why do we forget
  • Lack of attention
  • Not practicing enough
  • Lack of consolidation
  • Memory decay
  • Retrieval failure
