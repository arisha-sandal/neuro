What Is The Purpose Of Sleeping

Sleep gives your body the time to repair itself and carry out important functions,like clearing out waste and releasing hormones.Without these processes,our body can't function properly.

Some of the other bnefits of proper sleep are given below:-

  • Cellular Restoration
  • Weight Maintenance
  • Proper Insulin Function
  • Heart Health
  • Growth and healing
  • Improve Congnitive Function
  • Reduces stress

What happens in the brain when we sleep?

When we fall asleep,the brain does not merely go offline instead your brain cycles through active and resting stages to clean out waste,sort memories,restore energy and repair cellular connections.

Instead of shutting down, the brain performs high-precisison work into distinct categories.

  1. NREM Sleep
    • N1:Very Light Sleep - you are just starting to fall asleep.
    • N2: you become more deeply asleep,and our body relaxes.
    • N3:Deep Sleep: importat for physical recovery and restoration.
  1. REM Sleep
    • Brain activity becomes more active.
    • It is the stage where most dreams happen.
    • REM takes up about 25% of sleep,with shorter episodes early in the night and longer ones towars morning.
