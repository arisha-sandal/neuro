Some Fascinating Facts About Human Brain

  • The brain is the fattiest organ in the body, consisting of atleast 60% of weight.
  • About 70% of the brain is composed of water.
  • Brain activity never completely stops,even during deep sleep.
  • The human brain contains approximately 100 billion nerve cells, or neurons.
  • The brain can grow new connections and alter existing ones throughout life - a process called as neuroplasticity.

Main Parts Of The Brain and Their Functions
PARTS FUNCTIONS
Cerebrum The Cerebrum is the largest part of the brain , making up to about 80% to 85% of its total mass,it's main functionn is to control thinking,voluntary movement, speech and language, memory etc.
Cerebellum The Cerebellum often called as "little brain" coordinates voluntary muscle movements,maintains balance and posture and supports motor learning.
Brain Stem The Brain Stem connects your brain to spinal cord.It controls essential, automatic life support functions like breathing,heartrate and blood pressure.
